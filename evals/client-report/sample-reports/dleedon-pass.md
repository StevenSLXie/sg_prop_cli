## Unit Mix, Layout, And Stack/Site Considerations

URA rows do not include bedroom count, so exact 3-bedroom turnover cannot be calculated from URA alone. Any transaction segmentation by size should be labelled `area-band proxy, not verified bedroom count`. Before advising on a specific stack or unit, verify floor plan, balcony/void space, facing, afternoon sun, road noise, and whether the unit is a compact 3-bed or larger family layout.

## Liquidity And Turnover

Use project turnover proxy for now: 18 latest-12-month project transactions / 1,715 total units = about 1.0% annual project turnover. That is low to moderate liquidity. This is not layout turnover; final 3-bedder turnover needs a verified 3-bedroom unit-count denominator.

## Recommendation

D'LEEDON is worth shortlisting for a buyer who wants D10 scale, facilities, and a recognizable project, but it is not an automatic buy. The main caution is layout-specific liquidity: verify how many true 3-bedroom units exist, how often that exact layout trades, and whether current listings are asking a premium to recent evidence.

Who should avoid it: a buyer who wants boutique privacy, freehold tenure, or very high resale liquidity should compare alternatives before committing.
